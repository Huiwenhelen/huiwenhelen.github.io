代表案例Selected work

2025–2026 · Taipei · Osparks (Vpin)

從零建立創作者合作渠道，帶動新付費方案上線Building a creator partner channel from zero to launch a paid tier

背景Context
公司要推出 B2C Premium 訂閱方案，但沒有現成的推廣夥伴。The company was launching a B2C Premium subscription tier with no existing promotion partners.
做法What I did
自己找名單、陌生開發、提案到簽約，一路談成 YouTube 創作者／KOL 共同推廣；同時操盤中英文 Google Ads，並在展會現場做企業商務洽談。Sourced, cold-pitched, and signed YouTube creators/KOLs as co-promotion partners; ran Chinese/English Google Ads alongside, and led enterprise BD at trade expos.
成果Result
簽下 17 位以上創作者，上線 3 個月累積 90 位以上付費訂戶。17+ creators signed; 90+ paid subscribers within 3 months.
2018–2021 · Beijing & Taiwan · Caixin Global

重構訂閱商城，讓海外讀者付得了錢Rebuilding a subscription store so overseas readers could pay

背景Context
財新的海外讀者付款不便，網頁和 App 在海外也開得慢。Overseas readers struggled to pay, and the site and app loaded slowly outside China.
做法What I did
主導商城重構，接入 PayPal 與 Stripe，重建用戶中心並優化海外速度；另外開拓臉書社團、海外優惠券平台、新聞聚合平台等新獲客渠道，並建立增長與留存的追蹤報告。Led the rebuild, integrated PayPal and Stripe, rebuilt the user center, and sped up overseas access; opened new channels (Facebook groups, overseas coupon sites, news aggregators) and set up growth and retention reporting.
成果Result
續訂率 +700%、App 累積用戶 +166.78%、活躍用戶 +104.78%。Renewal rate +700%, cumulative app users +166.78%, active app users +104.78%.
2021–2023 · Remote · Initium Media

發行華語媒體第一個 NFTLaunching the first NFT from a Chinese-language newsroom

背景Context
端傳媒想試水 Web3，但華語媒體還沒有人發行過 NFT。Initium wanted to test Web3, and no Chinese-language media outlet had issued an NFT before.
做法What I did
從調研、商業評估、洽談 Web3 合作方，到經營媒體關係、發新聞稿，一手推到正式發行。同時推出新訂閱線「端 Plus」，並接入海外與加密貨幣支付。Took "The Erased" from research and business case through Web3 partner talks, media relations, and the press release to launch. In parallel, launched the "Initium Plus" tier and added overseas and crypto payments.
成果Result
「失去的____」成為華語媒體首發 NFT，獲得產業媒體報導。"The Erased" became the first NFT from a Chinese-language outlet and drew industry coverage.
2023–2025 · Taipei / Nanjing · Digiwin

帶兩岸團隊把 AI 助理做成可以賣的產品Turning internal AI assistants into products with a cross-strait team

背景Context
公司內部開發的 AI 工具，需要跨部門整合才能對外銷售。AI tools built in-house needed cross-team work before they could be sold.
做法What I did
帶領 20-50 人的兩岸團隊，協調 PaaS 與 SaaS 部門，建構差旅、外勤、出勤三款 AI 助理；並以資本市場角度向股東與投資人簡報成果。Led a 20–50 person team across Taiwan and Nanjing, coordinating PaaS and SaaS units on three AI assistants (travel, field service, attendance), and presented results to shareholders and investors.
成果Result
三款 AI 助理完成建構並推向企業客戶銷售。Three AI assistants built and brought to enterprise customers.
