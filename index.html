KOL 開發Creator sourcing

為 AI 影音知識庫 Vpin 從 0 開始，pitch 500 位創作者，簽約 17 位以上科技、財經、說書、建築領域的專家 KOL。Built the creator pipeline for Vpin, an AI video knowledge base, from scratch: pitched 500 creators and signed 17+ experts in tech, finance, book reviews and architecture.

全網覆蓋Reach

合作 KOL 覆蓋百萬以上 YouTube 追蹤者，並搭配中英文 Google Ads 與產業展會擴大觸及。Partner creators' channels reach over one million YouTube followers; bilingual Google Ads and industry expos extended that reach further.

粉絲轉換Fan conversion

把 KOL 的粉絲轉為 Vpin 用戶，推動 B2C Premium 付費方案上市。Turned creators' audiences into Vpin users and drove the launch of the B2C Premium tier.

成果Outcome

Pitch 500 位創作者、簽約 17+ 位專家 KOL；方案上市三個月內累積 90+ 位付費訂戶。500 creators pitched and 17+ expert creators signed; 90+ paid subscribers within three months of launch.

產品研究與重構Product research & rebuild

統籌訂閱業務的產品研究、建置與優化，涵蓋訂閱頁功能、支付系統與用戶流程：重構訂閱商城、接入 PayPal 與 Stripe 等海外第三方支付、建置用戶中心、改版新聞首頁、提升並監測海外存取速度，並優化 App。Led product research, development and optimization across the subscription business, covering subscription-page features, payment systems and user flow: rebuilt the subscription store, integrated overseas third-party payment providers such as PayPal and Stripe, built the user center, redesigned the news homepage, improved and monitored overseas access speed, and optimized the app.

數據追蹤體系Data tracking

重整新聞訂閱 App 業務的增長與留存報表，建立業務數據追蹤方法，用數據指導策略、達成營運目標；並結合時事熱點，與編輯部、產品與行銷團隊協作。Reorganized growth and retention reporting for the news subscription app business, and built a data-tracking methodology to guide strategy and hit operating goals. Worked with the newsroom, product and marketing teams to capitalize on trending news.

外部資源開拓External channels

研究並拓展臉書社團、海外優惠券平台、海外線上市場與新聞聚合平台：在聖誕與新年檔期從優惠券平台帶進外部訂戶，並把 Caixin Global 推廣給臉書社團用戶。Researched and developed external channels, including Facebook groups, overseas coupon platforms, overseas online marketplaces and news aggregators. Coupon platforms brought in new subscribers over the Christmas and New Year holidays, and Facebook groups introduced Caixin Global to new readers.

成果Outcome

訂閱續訂率提升 700%；App 累積用戶成長 166.78%，活躍用戶成長 104.78%。Subscription renewal rate up 700%, cumulative app users up 166.78%, and active app users up 104.78%.
