挑戰Challenge

策略Approach

主導訂閱商城重構，整合 PayPal 與 Stripe，重建用戶中心並提升海外存取速度；同時拓展社群、海外優惠平台與新聞聚合等獲客管道，並建立增長與留存的數據追蹤機制。Led the store rebuild, integrating PayPal and Stripe, rebuilding the user center and improving overseas performance; opened channels across social communities, coupon platforms and news aggregators, and set up growth and retention tracking.

成果Outcome

續訂率提升 700%；App 累積用戶成長 166.78%，活躍用戶成長 104.78%。Renewal rate up 700%, cumulative app users up 166.78%, and active users up 104.78%.
